The sticker audit

Above 75 F the melt quickens - 8 percent gone per 5 degrees - and every lid open costs 2 percent of a sticker day. The lab never met your beach.

The 2:1 rule

Twice the ice as contents, by weight. The rule counts the beer too, and a warm six-pack spends its first hour paying the ice back.

The block truth

One solid block outlasts the same pounds of cubes by half again. Same ice, fewer edges - melt is a surface-area business.

What it never does

It never quotes the roto-molded marketing, never sells you a second cooler, and never forgets the pre-chill. The derates are labeled norms; your meltwater keeps the honest ledger.

Cooler Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
